Practical farm solutions

The right machine for every problem in the field

Late sowing, hard soil, tight harvest windows and costly seed waste are common challenges. Here is how our machines and services help solve them.

Common challenges

Problems we help solve

Find the challenge closest to your situation and see which service fits.

Sowing is running late

Labour shortages or a delayed start can push sowing past the ideal window.

Our solution

Tractor service for ploughing and field preparation, paired with manual seed sowing machines to speed up planting.

Book tractor and sowing

Soil is hard or compacted

Hard, compacted ground makes it difficult for seeds to establish and roots to spread.

Our solution

Cultivator service to loosen and level the soil before sowing, giving seeds a better start.

Book cultivator

Harvest timing is tight

When the crop is ready, it needs to come in quickly before weather or quality changes.

Our solution

Crop harvesting service that brings in the crop efficiently, scheduled around your harvest date.

Book harvesting

Produce is hard to move

Carrying harvested produce from the field to storage or market takes time and effort.

Our solution

Sturdy trolleys for moving produce from the field to the market, combined with our tractor service.

Book trolley

Seed is wasted and spacing is uneven

Hand sowing often leads to uneven plant spacing and extra seed used per acre.

Our solution

Manual seed sowing machines that help place seeds more precisely, for even planting and less waste.

Book seed sowing
